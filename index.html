How to use
Check in and outTap the button on the Today tab when you arrive and when you leave campus.
Add a classUse "+ Class": pick the batch, timing and topic, and mark what you gave (worksheet, test, homework).
Add an activityFor anything that isn't a class, like meetings, leave or other work.
HistorySee any past day, week or month, and download or share a report.
ScheduleSet weekly class reminders and keep personal photos for quick reference.
